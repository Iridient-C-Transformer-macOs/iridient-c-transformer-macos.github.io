How It Works

Step 2: Choose Settings

Configure demosaicing (HiFi detail+ or smooth), sharpening, noise reduction, and lens corrections. Fine-tune for your workflow.

Step 3: Convert to DNG

Process with optimized algorithms. Output DNGs ready for Lightroom, Capture One, or any DNG-compatible editor.
